## Maker and taker fees explained

A **taker order** immediately matches an existing order on the order book. A **maker order** adds liquidity to the order book and may execute later.

OKX calculates spot and margin trading fees based on the fee rate multiplied by the amount bought or sold when the order fills. Futures fees use the contract specifications, number of contracts, contract size, and fill price.

For example, using the Regular US fee rates shown above:

- A $1,000 taker spot trade at 0.3500% would have a trading fee of about $3.50 before any applicable adjustments.
- A $1,000 maker spot trade at 0.2000% would have a trading fee of about $2.00 before any applicable adjustments.

These are simple illustrations, not guaranteed quotes for every pair. Actual fees can vary by instrument, market group, fee tier, and the account rate displayed at the time of the order.

The fee schedule also does not tell you the complete cost of a trade. You should consider:

- Bid-ask spread.
- Funding fees for perpetual futures.
- Network or withdrawal charges.
- Conversion pricing.
- Slippage on less liquid markets.
- Any reward restrictions attached to bonus funds.
